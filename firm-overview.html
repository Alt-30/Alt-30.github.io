▲ Alt-30 · Firm Overview01 · Who We Are
Who We Are

About the name,
and the practice.

Hold Alt and type 30. A keyboard returns ▲ — the oldest notation in economics for a quantity that has moved.

That is the whole of the work. A tax cut moves a general fund. A plant closure moves a county payroll. A development moves a city revenue base for thirty years.

Every question a client brings us is finally a question about how much moved, in which direction, and for whom.

We are an economic consulting firm producing the impact studies, fiscal analyses, revenue forecasts, and expert testimony that public and private decision-makers rely on when a policy, a project, or a budget is on the table.

We maintain our own records of appropriations, tax filings, and employment across fifty states and more than five hundred municipalities, back to fiscal year 1967, because measuring a change requires knowing what preceded it.

A PhD economist leads each engagement and presents the findings. We do not subcontract the analysis, and we do not send someone else to defend it.

We began in Arizona and keep unusual depth in that market. The method is identical in every state we cover, which is what lets one finding be compared against another.

We do not lobby, poll, or advise on message. We hold no lobbying registrations and take no position on legislation, candidates, or ballot measures. We produce the number and defend it in the room.

▲ Alt-30 · Firm Overview03 · How We Help
How We Help

Four stages,
one standard of proof.

An engagement is scoped and priced before work begins. What varies is the question. What does not vary is that every assumption is stated, every input is traceable, and the economist who built the model is the one who presents it.

01 / Define
Frame the Question
We establish what decision the analysis has to support, what would change the answer, and what standard of evidence the room will demand. Scope, deliverable, and fee are fixed here.
02 / Measure
Build the Model
Inputs come from primary filings and are never carried forward from an earlier study without re-verification. Multipliers and elasticities are chosen on the record and disclosed in the report.
03 / Deliver
Present the Finding
Findings arrive formatted for the venue that will receive them: a committee, a council, a board, or a newsroom. The economist who built the model takes the questions.
04 / Maintain
Keep It Current
Most studies are filed and immediately begin to age. Ours can be maintained as a living portal that updates as new filings post, so the finding is current the next time it is needed.

▲ Alt-30 · Firm Overview04 · Service Lines
Service Lines

Six services.
Scoped and priced in advance.

01
Economic Impact Analysis
What a project, employer, or policy contributes to an economy: output, earnings, and employment, direct and indirect, with every multiplier stated on the page.
02
Fiscal Impact Analysis
What a decision does to a public budget. Revenue and expenditure effects modeled by fund and by year, across the horizon a council or a legislature plans against.
03
Tax Policy Analysis
Incidence, elasticity, and revenue consequence of a proposed change: who pays it, how behavior responds, and what the treasury collects once it does.
04
Revenue Forecasting
Baseline and scenario forecasts for general funds, shared-revenue distributions, and dedicated streams, rebuilt as conditions change rather than once a year.
05
Expert Testimony
Findings presented to a committee, a council, a board, or a newsroom by the economist who produced them, and defended under questioning.
06
Living Data Portals
A maintained portal that updates as new filings post, so an analysis stays current after delivery. Access-controlled, white-labeled, built for a client team to use directly.

▲ Alt-30 · Firm Overview05 · Who We’ve Helped
Who We’ve Helped

Different clients.
The same question.

Our clients want different things from government. What none of them can do without is a credible answer to how much a decision moves, in which direction, and for whom.

01 / Government Relations & Lobbying Firms
A position advanced on assertion gets corrected in public; a position carrying an impact study does not. We are the economics bench behind government relations practices, producing analysis a firm presents to a committee under its own name.
02 / Campaigns, PACs & Legislative Caucuses
Candidates are held to economic claims about the district they seek to represent, and rarely have staff able to verify them. We build the district profile and check the claim before it is published rather than after.
03 / Corporate Procurement & Health IT
Whether a solicitation is genuinely funded is a question of appropriations, not procurement language. We analyze agency budget conditions, obligation timelines, and the tax exposure that follows fiscal stress.
04 / Municipalities & Local Government
Most towns depend on shared revenue they neither levy nor can forecast, and cannot fund an economist to model it. We serve as fractional economic staff: distribution scenarios, revenue alternatives, and the analysis officials carry to the statehouse.

How to Work With Us

How the work
is structured.

Conflicts

Start with one question. Most relationships begin with a single brief on one jurisdiction or one bill — enough to judge the standard of the work before committing to more.

Scope precedes price. We define the question, the deliverable, and the fee before analysis begins. No invoice is shaped by how long the work turned out to take.

Retainers follow the calendar. Session-length and annual relationships are structured around the legislative and budget cycles a client operates on, with rapid-response capacity during session.

The method does not change with the client. We are engaged by organizations on opposite sides of the same question, and the analysis is built the same way for each.

First step. Send the question in front of you. We will prepare a brief on the jurisdiction, agency, or bill you name before we speak, so the first conversation begins with evidence rather than a description of our services.
