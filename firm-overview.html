▲ Alt-30 · Firm Overview01 · Who We Are
Who We Are

About the name,
and the practice.

Hold Alt and type 30. A keyboard returns ▲, the oldest notation in economics for a quantity that has moved.

That is the whole of the work. A tax cut moves a general fund. A plant closure moves a county payroll. A development moves a city revenue base for thirty years.

An intelligence function exists to catch that movement early and say what it means. Every question a client brings us is finally a question about how much moved, in which direction, and for whom.

We provide external intelligence for organizations navigating economic, workforce, and public-sector change: continuous monitoring, custom research, and executive-ready reporting, without adding a position to your organization.

We maintain our own records of appropriations, tax filings, and employment across fifty states and more than five hundred municipalities, back to fiscal year 1967, because measuring a change requires knowing what preceded it.

A PhD economist leads each engagement and presents the findings. We do not subcontract the analysis, and we do not send someone else to defend it.

We began in Arizona and keep unusual depth in that market. The method is identical in every state we cover, which is what lets one finding be compared against another.

We are specialists, not a dashboard vendor. Economic data, workforce, public finance, government, and local markets are a combination most research firms do not carry. We hold no lobbying registrations and take no position on legislation, candidates, or ballot measures.

▲ Alt-30 · Firm Overview03 · How We Help
How We Help

We Monitor. We Analyze.
You Decide.

Most organizations have questions about the economy, the labor market, or the public sector that no one inside has time to answer properly. Alt-30 is that function, kept outside the building. Clients receive the following, configured to their geographies and industries at the outset.

Continuous Market Monitoring Standing coverage of the conditions, industries, and geographies your organization depends on, reported as they move.

Custom Economic Research A defined question, investigated properly and answered in writing, when the topic is too specific for anything off the shelf.

Workforce Intelligence Employment, wages, occupations, and labor-force movement resolved to the places you hire, sell, or govern.

Competitive & Market Analysis How a market, a region, or a competitor set is changing, and what it implies for the decision in front of you.

Policy & Regulatory Monitoring The legislative, budgetary, and regulatory changes that reach your organization, tracked before they become news and explained in terms of what they cost.

Executive-Ready Reports Findings written for the person who has to decide, with the source and the assumption on the page.

On-Demand Research A question sent on Tuesday, answered by someone who already knows your organization and already has the data open.

▲ Alt-30 · Firm Overview04 · Three Applications
Three Applications

Where the intelligence
gets used.

The same function serves three kinds of need. Two are standing programs of monitoring and reporting. The third is a scoped research engagement for a question that does not fit either.

01 / Workforce Intelligence
Know where the labor market is moving before your competitors do.
Monthly and quarterly intelligence on employment, unemployment, wages, occupations, labor force, industry shifts, demographics, and geographic differences, for staffing firms, workforce organizations, economic-development groups, and major employers.
02 / Municipal Intelligence
A continuously updated view of your community’s economic and fiscal environment.
Revenue, state-shared revenues, workforce, population, development, housing, tax base, peer communities, and fiscal resilience, with the Economic Resilience Index and the City Leader Portal as products inside the function.
03 / Custom Intelligence
Something your team doesn’t have time to investigate? We’ll research it.
Market expansion analysis, economic impact analysis, workforce studies, policy impact research, competitor and market research, custom dashboards, and executive research briefs. A defined question, a fixed fee, a finding.

▲ Alt-30 · Firm Overview05 · Who We’ve Helped
Who We’ve Helped

Different organizations.
The same problem.

Each has a continuous need for economic, workforce, or public-sector intelligence, and no one on staff free to produce it. The question differs by organization; the gap does not.

01 / Workforce Organizations & Employers
Staffing and recruiting firms deciding where to invest sales effort. Workforce boards and economic-development groups reporting to a board on regional conditions. Employers planning hiring, compensation, or a new location.
02 / Municipalities & Local Government
Rural and mid-sized towns and cities whose general fund depends on revenue they do not control, and whose councils want to see a shift before the budget cycle exposes it.
03 / Government Relations, Associations & Vendors
Firms that need an impact study behind a position. Associations whose members expect an answer with a source. Companies selling into government that need to know whether a program is actually funded.
04 / Campaigns & Legislative Caucuses
Candidates and caucuses held to economic claims about a district, who need the profile built and the claim checked before it is published rather than after.

Need an insights analyst, but not a full-time employee? Alt-30 provides the research capacity, analytical expertise, and ongoing monitoring without adding another position to your organization.

Scope precedes price. We define the question, the deliverable, and the fee before analysis begins. No invoice is shaped by how long the work turned out to take.

Retainers follow the calendar. Session-length and annual relationships are structured around the legislative and budget cycles a client operates on, with rapid-response capacity during session.

The method does not change with the client. We are engaged by organizations on opposite sides of the same question, and the analysis is built the same way for each.

First step: tell us what you need to know. Describe the question in front of your organization. We will tell you plainly whether it is something we can investigate, what the work would involve, and what it would cost.
