Practice  —  Government Relations & Lobbying Firms

Substantiating the argument before it is heard.

Firms are hired for judgment and access. They are retained on evidence. Alt-30 serves as the analytical function behind government relations practices in Arizona — testing positions against the empirical record, and preparing the exhibits that carry a firm's own name into committee.

The Situation

Committees have grown
harder to persuade.

Legislative staff now arrive at hearings with fiscal notes, agency data, and the capacity to check a claim in the time it takes to make one. A position advanced on assertion is corrected in public; a position advanced on the record is not.

Few firms can carry that analytical weight in-house, and fewer clients will pay for it directly.

Alt-30 is retained to close that gap — a research function shared across the practices that need it, at a fraction of the cost of building one.

01
An Analytical Function on CallA standing relationship gives the firm access to econometric models of Arizona workforce, tax, and budget data without adding headcount or carrying an analyst between sessions.
02
Positions Tested in AdvanceWe examine a policy argument against the record before it reaches committee. Where a claim will not hold, the firm learns it in advance of the hearing rather than during it.
03
Delivered Under Your NameExhibits, briefs, and model output are prepared for the firm to present as its own work. The client sees the firm's capability; our involvement need not appear.
04
No Conflict IntroducedAlt-30 holds no lobbying registrations and advocates for no legislation. Bringing us into an engagement raises no conflict, whatever the client roster or the coalition.
