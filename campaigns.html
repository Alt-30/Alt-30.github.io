Practice  —  Campaigns, PACs & Legislative Caucuses

Every district has an economy. Few campaigns can describe it.

Candidates are asked what has happened to wages, employment, and taxes in the place they seek to represent, and are held to the answer. Alt-30 prepares current economic profiles for legislative and congressional districts nationwide, and reviews the claims a campaign intends to make before they are published.

The Situation

Statewide talking points
lose to local specifics.

The economic argument that persuades a district is rarely the one that persuades a state. It concerns a particular levy, a particular employer, a particular year in which something changed — and it is checkable.

Campaigns are seldom staffed to assemble that record, and never on the timeline a debate imposes.

Alt-30 maintains that record continuously across all fifty states, which is what allows us to answer within a cycle rather than a quarter.
