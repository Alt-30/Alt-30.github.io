Workforce

You hire against a labor market you cannot see until the data is stale.

Staffing firms, employers, and economic-development teams make placement, pay, and location decisions on figures that are a quarter old by the time they are official. The opening you were chasing has often already closed.

How we clear it. We monitor employment, wages, and occupations resolved to the geographies you actually operate in, refreshed on the federal release cycle, and we carry AI-exposure signals most dashboards do not.

Municipal

Your general fund rides on revenue you cannot forecast or defend.

Most towns depend on state-shared revenue they neither levy nor control, and are asked to defend it at the statehouse without an economist on staff. The shift usually shows up in the budget cycle before anyone sees it coming.

How we clear it. We monitor the fiscal picture continuously, score resilience against genuine peers rather than the nearest large city, and build the scenarios an official can carry into a hearing.

Associations and government relations

You argue positions you cannot substantiate fast enough to stay credible.

When a member or a committee asks for the number behind a position, the answer has to be sourced and it has to be quick. Slow or soft evidence loses the room, and a figure no one can trace is worse than none.

How we clear it. We produce independent, sourced analysis on the questions your field actually argues, and we publish it openly through PoliMetrics so it carries weight when you cite it.
