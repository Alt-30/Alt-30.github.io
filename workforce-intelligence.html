Workforce Intelligence

Know where the labor market is moving before your competitors do.

Monthly and quarterly intelligence on employment, wages, occupations, and industry shifts, resolved to the geographies your organization actually hires in, sells into, or governs, and delivered in a form an executive can act on the same day.

What we monitor

The labor market moves locally, and it moves first.

National figures describe a country. The decision in front of you concerns a metro, a county, an occupation, and a quarter. By the time the local picture reaches a news story, the opening it described has usually closed.

▲
Employment and unemployment
Payroll employment, unemployment rates, and claims activity for the metros, counties, and states you operate in, reported on the federal release cycle.
▲
Wages
Wage levels and wage growth by occupation and industry, so a compensation decision or a bid rate rests on the current local figure rather than a national average.
▲
Occupations and labor force
Occupational demand, labor-force participation, and the supply of specific skills in a region. The difference between a market that is tight and one that is merely loud.
▲
Industry shifts
Which industries are adding or shedding work in your geographies, and what that implies for hiring, placement, and pricing.
▲
Demographics
How the population of a region is changing, by age, education, and migration, and what that means for the workforce behind your growth plans.
▲
Geographic differences
Your markets set against each other and against comparable regions, so an expansion, a closure, or a recruiting push targets the place the data supports.

Reported on a cadence. Answered on demand.
