The Urban Revenue Sharing Problem

When the Capitol debates income tax,
your general fund is on the table.

Arizona's Urban Revenue Sharing program ties municipal general funds directly to state income tax collections. Every time state leaders discuss cutting the income tax or reworking the distribution formula, they are — whether they realize it or not — debating the financial survival of towns like Show Low, Snowflake, and Payson.

A full-time chief economist costs $130,000 a year. The legislative session that threatens your URS allocation doesn't care that you can't afford one.

Alt-30's Municipal Partnerships & Research wing exists to close that gap: a fractional Chief Economist & Budget Director, shared across rural Arizona.
