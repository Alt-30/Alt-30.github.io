Application  —  Municipal Intelligence

Give leadership a continuously updated view of your community’s economic and fiscal environment.

Leadership rarely lacks data. It lacks someone watching it. Municipal Intelligence gives a council, a manager, and a finance director one maintained picture of revenue, workforce, population, development, and the peer communities they are measured against — and someone to explain what moved.

What We Monitor

When a statehouse debates income tax,
it is debating your general fund.

Most states return a share of income or sales tax collections to their municipalities. Arizona calls it Urban Revenue Sharing; the label differs by state, the exposure does not. A town receives a share of a revenue stream it does not levy, cannot forecast, and has no standing to protect.

A full-time chief economist costs upward of $130,000 a year. A town of nine thousand cannot carry one, and the session does not adjust for that.

Municipal Intelligence is that function, shared across the communities that need it: continuous monitoring of the conditions a council governs, and analysis when one of them moves.
