Why the Numbers Hold

An estimate is only as good
as the room it survives.

Any consultant can produce a favorable figure. The question that decides whether it is worth anything is what happens when a skeptical committee member, an opposing economist, or a reporter asks how it was derived.

We build every analysis to answer that question.

Assumptions are stated in the report. Multipliers are disclosed. Every input traces to a primary filing, and the model is available for inspection by anyone who cares to check it.

01
Stated AssumptionsMultipliers, elasticities, and growth rates appear in the report rather than in a file we keep. A reader can disagree with an assumption and see precisely what changing it would do.
02
Primary SourcesEvery input traces to an appropriation, a tax roll, or a federal labor release. Nothing is carried forward from an earlier study without re-verification.
03
Consistent MethodThe same construction in every jurisdiction, which is what allows one state's finding to be compared against another's — and what keeps our own numbers consistent between engagements.
04
Presented in PersonThe economist who built the model presents it and takes the questions. An analysis that cannot be defended out loud is not finished.

The Firm

About the name,
and the firm.

Hold Alt and type 30. A keyboard returns ▲ — the oldest notation in economics for a quantity that has moved.

That is the whole of the work. A tax cut moves a general fund. A plant closure moves a county payroll. A development moves a city revenue base for thirty years. Every question a client brings us is finally a question about how much moved, in which direction, and for whom.

Alt-30 is an economic consulting firm built to answer those questions with numbers that hold up. We maintain longitudinal records of appropriations, tax filings, and employment across fifty states and more than five hundred municipalities, back to fiscal year 1967, because measuring a change requires knowing what preceded it. The firm began in Arizona and keeps unusual depth there; the method is identical in every state we cover.

A PhD economist leads each engagement. We do not lobby, we do not poll, and we do not advise on message. We produce the number, and we defend it in the room.
