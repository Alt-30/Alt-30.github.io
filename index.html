The Firm

Alt-30 is an Arizona strategic intelligence firm built around a single asset: proprietary data infrastructure we constructed ourselves.

Our econometric models integrate five decades of appropriations, tax, and employment records across Arizona's 15 counties, 91 municipalities, and all 30 legislative districts — cross-referenced with federal Census, labor, and fiscal data. The name is the mandate: every one of Arizona's 30 districts, covered.

A PhD economist stands behind every engagement, and we publish independent research through PoliMetrics, our platform covering state fiscal policy, labor economics, and policy diffusion.
