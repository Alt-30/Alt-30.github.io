About Alt-30

Independent analysis.
No conflicts. No platforms.

Alt-30 Consulting is an independent government risk intelligence firm built around a single differentiator: we built our own data infrastructure from scratch.

Our proprietary longitudinal database covers 500+ municipalities and all 50 states from fiscal year 1967 to present, integrated with Census, FRED, DOL, and state appropriations data. That infrastructure lets us show clients what a policy trajectory means before it shows up in a budget shortfall or a political crisis.

We carry a PhD economist on every engagement, operate with no vendor relationships, and publish independent research through PoliMetrics, our research platform covering state fiscal policy, labor economics, and federal policy diffusion.

We serve both sides of the policy table — the governments setting policy and the organizations navigating its consequences.
