The firm

About the name, and the firm.

Hold Alt and type 30. A keyboard returns ▲, the oldest notation in economics for a quantity that has moved.

That is what an intelligence function exists to catch. A shift in the labor market. A change in a shared-revenue formula. A competitor entering a region. A budget line that quietly disappeared. Each is a question of how much moved, in which direction, and what it means for the organization watching.

Alt-30 is built to be that function for organizations that cannot justify staffing it. We maintain longitudinal records of appropriations, tax filings, and employment across fifty states and more than five hundred municipalities, back to fiscal year 1967, because noticing a change requires knowing what came before it. The firm began in Arizona and keeps unusual depth there. The method is identical in every state we cover.

A PhD economist leads each engagement. We do not lobby, we do not poll, and we do not advise on message. We tell you what is happening, and we show our work.
